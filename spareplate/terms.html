3. Boards

Whether you are near a building is checked on your device, and your location is not sent to us; see our Privacy Policy for how location is handled.

Boards are open to whoever can reach them. If you share an invite code, anyone holding that code who is also near the building can join and see everything posted on that board. 6. Voting and reporting

Voting. Anyone on a board can mark someone else's post "Still There" or "Gone". You get one vote per post, you can change it, and you cannot vote on your own posts. A post is only marked as probably gone once several members agree. Do not manipulate votes — coordinating votes to bury a legitimate post, or to keep a stale one alive, is misuse.

Duplicate posts. If you post food that someone has already posted about in the same place, the app may turn your post into a "Still There" vote on the existing post rather than creating a second one.

Reporting. Every post made by someone else can be reported. Reporting does two things: it hides that post on your device immediately and permanently, and it files a report with us for review. The hide is local and cannot be undone from within the app — other members continue to see the post unless and until we remove it from the server. Please report anything that breaks section 5; do not use reports to settle disagreements or to hide posts you simply dislike.

7. Your content, and who can remove it

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to store and display your content to other users of the app so the service can function, for as long as the post exists.

Leaving the feed is not deletion. A post is treated as expired about 2 hours after it goes up, and stops appearing on the board about 6 hours after it goes up. Both of these only change what the app shows you. The post itself — including the name on it and any photo — stays stored on the server until something actually deletes it.

Four things actually delete a post, and you should understand all of them before you post:

  • You delete the post yourself. This removes it from the server for everyone, not just from your screen, and it works whether or not the post has already left the feed. You can also edit your own posts while they are active.
  • The creator of the board deletes the board. Only the creator can do this. It permanently erases the board and every post on it, including yours, for all members. It cannot be undone.
  • The board is treated as abandoned. A board with no posts for roughly two months stops appearing in searches and is deleted from the server, along with its posts (deletion completes the next time the board creator's device runs the app; until then the board is hidden from all users).
  • We remove it, as described in section 8.

Removing a board from your own list is different from all of the above. It affects only your device: the board and its posts continue to exist for everyone else, and you can rejoin by returning to the building (and entering the invite code, if the board requires one).
